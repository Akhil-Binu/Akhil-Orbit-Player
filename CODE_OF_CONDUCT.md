# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing an open, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for all contributors, maintainers, and users of the Orbit Player project, regardless of background, identity, or technical experience.

---

## Expected Standards

Participants in the project are expected to:
* Demonstrate empathy, kindness, and mutual respect in discussions and code reviews.
* Offer constructive, actionable feedback on technical proposals and pull requests.
* Focus on what is best for the project community, end-user privacy, and application quality.
* Gracefully accept constructive criticism and respect differing technical perspectives.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated:
* Harassment, derogatory commentary, or personal insults.
* Trolling, deliberate disruption, or offensive conduct in issues or pull requests.
* Publishing private personal information of others without explicit consent.
* Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a collaborative professional context.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate or harmful.

To report violations of these standards, please contact the repository maintainer directly at **AkhilBinuRoom@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated confidentially.
